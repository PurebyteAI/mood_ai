Zero-Storage Architecture

Your reflections belong to you alone. We engineered AI Mood Mirror without user accounts, databases, or tracking:

  • No User Account: You never register, log in, or give your identity.
  • Ephemeral Memory: Your written thoughts and drawings live solely inside your active browser tab's RAM.
  • Zero Persistent Storage: When you close the browser tab, your session is erased permanently.
  • Encrypted in Transit: Communication with AI analysis engines happens via stateless HTTPS requests.
